# Final Business Insights and Recommendations

# Overview

This project analyzed Google Play Store reviews from:

- Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)
- Bank of Abyssinia (BOA)
- Dashen Bank

The analysis focused on:
- customer sentiment
- recurring complaints
- feature requests
- overall customer experience

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)

### Satisfaction Drivers
- Fast and reliable money transfers
- Simple user interface

### Pain Points
- Login failures
- Slow loading during transactions

### Recommendations
1. Improve backend transaction processing speed
2. Strengthen authentication reliability and OTP delivery

---

## 2. Bank of Abyssinia (BOA)

### Satisfaction Drivers
- Easy account access
- Mobile banking convenience

### Pain Points
- Frequent crashes
- Poor customer support response

### Recommendations
1. Improve app stability and crash monitoring
2. Introduce AI chatbot support for faster issue resolution

---

## 3. Dashen Bank

### Satisfaction Drivers
- Good UI design
- Positive transfer experience

### Pain Points
- Slow app updates
- Feature limitations

### Recommendations
1. Add fingerprint authentication
2. Improve app performance optimization

---

# Cross-Bank Findings

## Common Issues
The following issues appeared repeatedly across all three banks:
- slow transaction processing
- login and OTP issues
- app crashes
- delayed updates

## Common Positive Themes
- convenience
- easy transfers
- mobile accessibility

---

# Strategic Recommendations

## Product Improvements
- Optimize transaction APIs
- Improve server reliability
- Add biometric authentication
- Reduce app loading time

## Customer Support Improvements
- Deploy AI-powered customer support chatbot
- Improve issue escalation workflows
- Enhance OTP reliability

---

# Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that customer reviews provide highly valuable signals about fintech product quality and user satisfaction.

Banks that actively monitor customer sentiment and address recurring complaints can improve:
- customer retention
- mobile banking adoption
- competitive advantage
